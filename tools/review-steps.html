Application sheet — all 10 steps

Every step of the application ported from apply.capella.edu. Each frame loads the homepage with ?step=N, which opens the sheet directly at that step — including the conditional ones you cannot reach by clicking.

Prototype only. Steps 3–5 and 9 reproduce the real application's date-of-birth, last-four-SSN and password fields. There is no form action, no endpoint and no storage. Do not deploy this publicly and do not enter real personal data.
